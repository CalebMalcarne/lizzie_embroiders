A few helpful details

Good to know before you order.

Embroidery sizing & design limits +

Our current maximum embroidery area is approximately 4 × 4 inches (10 × 10 cm). It's perfect for small chest designs, patches, names, monograms, and other delicate accents. Very detailed photos, extremely small lettering, gradients, and oversized designs may need simplification or may not be possible.

Garments, blanks & sizing +

We can discuss available blank items such as Gildan 18000 crewneck sweatshirts ↗, Gildan 5000 T-shirts ↗, and Liberty Bags totes ↗. These are style references, not guaranteed stock. We confirm the exact item, color, measurements, pricing, and availability before your order is approved. When ordering clothes, please consult the manufacturer's size chart for the specific style; sizing may vary. Customer-supplied items are considered case by case.

Shipping & local meetups +

We ship to the 48 contiguous United States; shipping costs are paid by the customer and provided in your quote. We can also arrange an agreed public meetup between Waterbury and Hartford, Connecticut. A $25 minimum order applies to local public meetups. No home address or private pickup location is offered on this site.

Image uploads & artwork permissions +

Please upload clear, high-resolution artwork or a simple logo where possible. PNG, JPG, or PDF references are useful, but images must be converted into embroidery stitch files before production and may require an additional setup fee. By submitting artwork, you confirm that you own it or have permission to use and reproduce it. We cannot guarantee that every uploaded image can be embroidered.

Google Forms may require you to sign into a Google account to upload an image. If that is inconvenient, submit the request without artwork or email us the file instead.

Content & right to decline requests +

We reserve the right to decline any order or design that is unlawful, infringes intellectual property rights, contains hateful or discriminatory content, harassment, or explicit material, or that we're otherwise uncomfortable producing. This applies to images, text, and custom requests. Order acceptance remains at our discretion, subject to applicable law.

Quotes, timing & custom requests +

Submitting a request is free and does not obligate you to purchase. We'll confirm design feasibility, total price, estimated turnaround, and payment terms before accepting an order. Since every piece is custom, please wait for confirmation before assuming a deadline can be met. Have an unusual idea? Email Lizzie and we'll see what we can do.
